TIDAL Books – private test version 0.5.0-beta4

An unofficial Android audiobook app for your TIDAL account.
Requires Android 8 or newer, internet access and your own TIDAL account with a suitable subscription.
There are no offline downloads or library synchronization.

INSTALL
Open TIDAL-Books-0.5.0-beta4.apk on your phone and allow installation from your browser/file manager if prompted.
Install updates over the existing app. Do not uninstall or clear app data: listening progress is stored locally.

LANGUAGE
German and English are available. The app follows your system language by default.
To choose manually: Settings (Einstellungen) → Appearance (Darstellung) → Language (Sprache) → English.
The selection also applies to widgets and media controls. Book titles and your own bookmark names stay unchanged.

FIRST USE
1. Choose Connect to TIDAL in your library, then Connect to TIDAL in Settings.
2. Open Confirm on TIDAL, sign in to your own account and confirm the device code.
   Use the back gesture to return to the app. There is no automatic browser redirect.
3. Find an audiobook album in the official TIDAL app. Share its link with TIDAL Books,
   or copy the link and paste it using Add in your library.
4. Open the book and tap Play. On reopening the app, it stays paused at your saved position.

THINGS TO TRY
Playback, skipping, resuming after restarting, bookmarks, sleep timer, lock-screen controls,
both app designs, language switching and widgets (long-press → Options/Edit, depending on your launcher).
Widget design and opacity are independent of the app design and currently apply to all widgets.
Save a backup from Settings → Backup & restore before testing or updating if you wish.

FEEDBACK
Please include version 0.5.0-beta4, phone model, Android version and steps to reproduce.
For widget issues, include the launcher name. Do not share passwords, device codes or account details.

Your library, listening progress and bookmarks stay on your device. Sign-in is stored encrypted.
The APK does not contain the developer's personal account. This is not an official TIDAL product;
the unofficial integration may change or stop working.
